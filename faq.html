Orders & Payment

Buying, pricing, currency and receipts.

  • All digital product prices are shown and charged in US dollars (USD). If your card is issued in another currency, your bank will convert the amount at their prevailing rate and may add a foreign transaction fee.
  • We accept major international credit and debit cards through our payment provider. Card details are entered on the provider's secure hosted checkout — Emmanus Plus never sees or stores your full card number.
  • Yes. We accept international cards subject to our payment provider's supported countries and your card issuer's own controls.
  • Yes. An order and payment confirmation is emailed automatically to the address used at checkout, immediately after a successful payment.
  • Check your spam or junk folder first, and confirm the address you used at checkout. If it still has not arrived, contact us with the name and email used and we will resend it.
  • Yes, for corporate experiences and corporate licences. These are arranged directly with Emmanus Plus rather than through the website checkout — send us an enquiry and we will issue a quotation and invoice.

Product Access & Delivery

How you receive and open what you have bought.

  • Immediately. Once payment is confirmed, your access is issued automatically and an email is sent to the address used at checkout.
  • It is delivered as a protected digital system. You receive a licence that is unique to you, activated through our licensing provider's secure viewer. This protects the methodology, templates and tools that make up the system.
  • As a personalised PDF issued to your purchase email. It is intended to be printed for your own use or opened on a tablet and completed with a stylus.
  • Yes. Printing for your own personal use is permitted and expected — it is designed to be worked on by hand.
  • Access and printing for the Alignment System are governed by the licence issued with your purchase. Where printing is restricted, this is stated in your licence terms at the point of access.
  • Your licence covers your own use. Device limits, where they apply, are set out in the Digital Product Licence & Usage Terms and confirmed when your licence is issued.
  • Contact us from the email address used at purchase. We can reissue access or move your licence to a new device, subject to the terms of your licence.

Licence & Usage

What you may do with what you have bought.

  • No. A standard purchase is a single-user licence for your own use. Copies are identifiable to the purchaser. If you need your team to have access, a corporate licence is the correct route and is usually better value.
  • Not under a standard single-user licence. Facilitation and client-facing use require a corporate or facilitator licence agreed with Emmanus Plus.
  • Yes. Copies carry identifying information linked to the purchaser. This deters redistribution and lets us trace the source of any unauthorised copy.
  • Yes, where the licence terms are breached — for example redistribution or unauthorised commercial use. See the Digital Product Licence & Usage Terms.

Technical Support

Help with activation, access and files.

  • Purchases of the Business-to-People Alignment System™ include seven days of technical support from the date of purchase, covering licence activation, secure viewer installation and access problems. Support for The Executive Reset™ covers download and access issues by email.
  • Email us from the address you used at purchase and include your order reference. This lets us verify the purchase and resolve access issues quickly.
  • Support is handled during UK business hours, Monday to Friday. We aim to respond within one business day, and within two business days at the latest.
  • Confirm you have activated your licence using the code sent to your purchase email, and that you are opening the file with the licensed viewer rather than a standard PDF reader. If it still fails, send us the error message and we will help.

Corporate Experiences & Licensing

Facilitated sessions and team-wide access.

  • Send a corporate enquiry with your preferred format, approximate participant numbers and timing. We will come back with a quotation and available dates.
  • Standard experiences are designed for up to 20 participants. For 21 or more, or for multi-team and multi-region delivery, we design a custom experience.
  • Both. Tell us which you need in your enquiry and we will confirm what is possible for your dates and locations.
  • A corporate licence lets your own facilitators run Executive Reset™ sessions internally, using licensed materials and facilitator guidance, within agreed participant volumes and usage terms.

Refunds & Cancellations

Where refunds do and do not apply.

  • Because access is issued immediately and cannot be returned, digital product sales are final once access has been issued. We do refund duplicate charges, and failed deliveries we cannot resolve. Full detail is in our Refund Policy.
  • Contact us with both order references and we will refund the duplicate charge in full.
  • Yes, subject to the notice periods set out in your quotation and the Refund Policy.
  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Your bank typically takes five to ten business days to show the credit.

Seven-day technical support

Every purchase of the Business-to-People Alignment System™ includes seven days of technical support from the date of purchase, covering licence activation, secure viewer installation and access problems.

Access problems

Lost your link, changed device, or the viewer will not open your file? Email us from the address you bought with and quote your order reference — we can reissue access or move your licence.

Still stuck?

Write to info@avensraconsulting.com or use the contact form. We aim to respond within one business day.
