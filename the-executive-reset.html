The journey

Four movements, one sitting

Designed for someone with forty minutes and a lot on their mind — not for someone with a free afternoon.

  1. 01

    Release

    Put down the mental load you have been carrying between meetings. Nothing to solve yet — just somewhere to set it down.

  2. 02

    Reset

    Give your attention somewhere deliberate to land. The colouring is not decoration; it is what lets the thinking mind step back.

  3. 03

    Reflect

    Prompts that ask the questions a full calendar never leaves room for. Short enough to answer honestly.

  4. 04

    Return

    Come back to the work having decided what actually matters this week — not just what is loudest.
